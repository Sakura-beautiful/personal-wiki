## TL;DR

OpenVLA is an open-source 7B VLA model fine-tuned from Prismatic-7B on the Open X-Embodiment dataset. It demonstrates strong generalization across robot morphologies and tasks.

## Motivation

Proprietary VLA models (like RT-2) are not accessible for research. An open-source alternative enables the community to study, reproduce, and extend VLA research.

## Problem

How do you build a generalizable robot policy from internet-scale vision-language pretraining?
